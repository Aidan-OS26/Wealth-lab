How it works

Revenue = selling price × items sold.

Total costs = variable cost × items sold + fixed costs.

Profit = revenue − total costs.

Variable costs rise with each item sold—such as the product, packaging and per-sale fees. Fixed costs stay the same in this model, regardless of sales volume.

Try this: Increase your price from 60 to 70 lei. Then reduce sales from 20 to 15 items. Does your profit improve?
